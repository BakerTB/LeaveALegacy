To the Next Group

A Note From Me

PostAP is unlike any class you have taken. There are no rubrics, no tests — it is just you, a problem, and whatever drive you can pull together to figure it out. That is the terrifying and incredible part of this class. This class will take you as far as your imagination will permit.

The best thing I did was pick projects that genuinely interested me. When I hit walls, and I hit a lot of them, like teaching myself derivatives while still in Pre-Calc and post-graduate math to simulate black holes, curiosity kept me going way more effectively than any grade ever could have.

Skills compound here in a way they do not in normal classes. My trading bot got sharper because I understood numerical methods from the pendulum project. My black hole simulation became possible because I had already built the 2D lensing version first. Every project you finish makes the next one more possible. That is not a coincidence, that is the whole point of this class.

This class also opens real doors. My trading bot is actually profitable and something I plan to continue running and building into the future. I was offered an internship at a 300 million dollar hedge fund through work I did in this class. Investors have taken genuine interest in projects built in this room. These are not hypotheticals, they are things that happened because this class gives you the ability to explore. I did not realize how important this class was to me until I finally started seeing it slip away. I wish I had more time, because the greatest classes are the ones that keep you up until 4am, not for a grade, but because you are too engulfed in it to quit.

If I could talk to myself when I was first entering this class, I would tell him: pick problems you actually want solved. Do not build something you do not care about, because you will not grow that way. Have fun. This class is meant to be a unique experience that you walk away from as a better and more capable person.

— Truitt Baker, PostAP CS 2023–2026
