📡Triple Indicator Strategy

All three indicators must signal simultaneously for the bot to execute a trade. This requirement drastically reduces false signals — requiring confluence from three independent systems before risking capital.

RSI — Relative Strength Index
Momentum oscillator measuring speed and magnitude of recent price changes. Signals overbought (>70) and oversold (<30) conditions. Built from scratch.
Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
Price inefficiency zones created when a candle moves so fast it leaves a gap in price. The market often returns to fill these gaps — the bot identifies and trades this tendency.
S&R Bands
Dynamic bands drawn at prior session highs, lows, and closes. Price behavior near these levels has high predictive value. Custom indicator also published on TradingView.
