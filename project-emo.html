EMO Music
Classifier

A rule-based classifier that analyzes audio files and optional lyrics to determine if a song is emo — no training data required. Uses signal processing and NLP to compute an emo_percent score and boolean decision.

🌿Overview
→Given any audio file (.mp3/.wav), the classifier outputs an emo_percent score (0–100), a confidence percentage, and a boolean is_emo decision — without ever being trained on labeled song data.
→Works by extracting audio features with librosa — the same library used in music information retrieval research — and combining them with hand-crafted rules based on what makes a song sound "emo" (distorted guitars, minor keys, dynamic swings, fast drums).
→Optionally accepts a lyrics text file for a second scoring pass — analyzing first-person pronoun density, emo keyword frequency, and textual intensity markers (exclamation marks, ALL CAPS, repeated phrases).
→Optional baseline comparison: feed it a known emo song and a known non-emo song — the classifier boosts the score for songs that sound similar to the emo baseline and penalizes those resembling the non-emo baseline, using cosine similarity on combined feature vectors.
🎵Audio Feature Extraction

Every song is loaded at 22,050 Hz mono and analyzed across these signal-processing features, which are then combined into four composite scores:

Distortion Proxy
High spectral centroid + high rolloff + high ZCR + flat spectrum → guitar distortion. Weighted 45% in the audio score.
Drum Drive
Onset strength (attack density) + tempo centered at 155 BPM. Emo drums are fast and punchy. Weighted 25%.
Vocal Intensity Variance
Standard deviation of RMS and spectral centroid over time. High variance = dramatic vocal dynamics typical of emo. Weighted 30%.
Delta Energy
90th percentile RMS minus 10th percentile RMS — measures how much the song's energy swings between quiet and loud. Core emo structural marker. Weighted 70% of structure score.
Minor Key Score
Chroma analysis detects minor key strength (A minor + B minor chroma energy). Emo music overwhelmingly favors minor keys.
Chorus Repetition
Recurrence matrix on chroma vectors measures structural repetition — choruses create high off-diagonal recurrence. 30% of structure score.
📝Lyric Analysis

When a lyrics file is provided, five text features are extracted and combined into a lyric_score (0–1). This score then gets 50% weight in the final emo_percent calculation.

First-Person Density (30%)
Frequency of "I", "me", "my", "myself", "I'm". Emo songs are deeply personal and self-referential.
Emo Keyword Density (30%)
19 keywords: "miss", "broken", "alone", "numb", "worthless", "hurt", "death", "empty", "regret", and others normalized by word count.
Relational Words (20%)
"you/your/we/us" + romance words ("love", "heart", "apart", "forever"). Emo is defined by relationship-driven narratives.
Intensity Markers (10%)
Exclamation marks per line, ALL CAPS word count, and repeated bigrams — markers of emotional intensity in the text.
Negative Density (10%)
Subset of hardest keywords: "alone", "broken", "lost", "numb", "hurt", "cry", "worthless" — the darkest word cluster.
📊Scoring Pipeline
Final emo_percent calculation
With lyrics:
  emo_percent = 100 × (0.50 × lyric_score + 0.25 × structure_score + 0.25 × audio_score)

Audio only:
  emo_percent = 100 × (0.55 × structure_score + 0.45 × audio_score)

Consistency penalty:
  penalty = 1 − 0.4 × |structure_score − audio_score|
  emo_percent × penalty ← penalizes conflicting signals

Sigmoid calibration → confidence → final decision:
  emo_percent = sigmoid(emo_percent, midpoint=threshold, sharpness=0.15)
  confidence = |emo_percent − threshold| / 50
  decision_percent = (emo_percent + confidence) / 2
  is_emo = decision_percent > 50
⚙️Three Decision Profiles
Strict
63%
High threshold. Only songs with very strong emo signals across all features get flagged. Minimizes false positives.
Balanced
55%
Default profile. Moderate threshold with equal sensitivity to audio and structure. Best general use.
Lenient
48%
Lower threshold. Catches borderline emo/post-punk/screamo adjacent songs. Higher false positive rate.

Profile also adjusts audio_weights (distortion/vocal/drum ratios) and structure_weights (delta_energy/chorus ratios). All three are selectable via CLI flag: --profile strict

🔬Baseline Comparison Mode
→Optionally feed a known emo song (--baseline-audio EMOSong.mp3) and a known non-emo song (--anti-baseline-audio REALLYNONEMO.mp3).
→Both songs are converted to a combined feature vector: MFCC (20 coefficients) + chroma + spectral contrast + RMS + onset strength + tempo. Then cosine similarity is computed between the target song and each baseline.
→Contrast score = 0.5 + 0.5 × (emo_similarity − non_emo_similarity). Songs that sound more like the emo baseline than the non-emo baseline get boosted. The blend weight is adjustable (--baseline-weight 0.35).
