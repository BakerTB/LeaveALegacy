🌿Overview
→Programmed and designed a full Hangman game with a graphical GUI in Python — then built two different solver algorithms that can play and be watched in real time.
→The core question: how do you optimally solve Hangman? Is it pure letter frequency? Pattern matching? The project explores both methods and compares their performance.
→Built Fall 2024 — 3 hrs/week over 2 weeks.
🤖Two Solvers Compared
Method 01
Bot Solver
Uses letter frequency data from English word lists to rank guesses. Starts with the most common letters (E, T, A, O, I...) and works systematically through likelihood — no word-pattern context used.
Method 02
AI Solver
Pattern-aware solver that cross-references the known letter positions with a dictionary to narrow possible words at each step. Guesses based on which letters appear most often in the remaining candidate words — not just English overall.
🔬Why This Is a Real CS Problem
→Information theory at its core: Each guess reveals information about the hidden word. The optimal solver maximizes expected information gain per guess — exactly what the AI solver approximates.
→Search space reduction: The AI solver's power comes from pruning — with every reveal, the set of possible words shrinks dramatically. Smart guessing makes this shrink faster.
→The Bot vs. AI gap: The Bot's flaw is using global frequency data rather than the frequency within the filtered word set. The AI fixes this by recomputing per step.
→Real-time visualization: Watching both solvers play side-by-side in the GUI makes the strategy difference immediately visible — you see the AI narrow in faster.
