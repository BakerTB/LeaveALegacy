Excel Absence Tracker — How It Works
→Input: The master operations Excel sheet where team members log their schedules and planned absences.
→Processing: Python script reads every row, identifies absence flags, and sorts by date and team member — all without touching the original sheet.
→Output: A clean, auto-generated Excel file with all absence days listed, ready for managers to reassign project work.
→Impact: What previously took a manager 45+ minutes of manual searching and copying now happens in seconds on-demand.
Calendar Bot — How It Works
→Trigger: A new employee record is created in the company system — this event fires the Power Automate flow.
→Template logic: The flow reads from a pre-defined onboarding calendar template, filling in the new hire's details and required Zoom meeting links.
→Output: Calendar invites for all required onboarding calls are sent automatically — no manual scheduling by HR or managers.
→Impact: Saves approximately 20 minutes per new employee. Scales perfectly — adding more hires doesn't add more work.
