Poker Bot

An automated poker-playing program — currently under active development. Full details coming soon.

🌿What It Will Do
→A bot that plays poker automatically — making betting, folding, and raising decisions based on programmed strategy parameters.
→Draws on the same analytical thinking used in the Options Trading Bot — finding edge through systematic decision-making rather than intuition.
→Poker is a game of incomplete information — the bot must make decisions under uncertainty using expected value calculations and hand strength assessment.
→Currently in active development. Full project writeup will be added when complete.
🧠The Challenge of Poker AI
→Expected Value (EV): Every action in poker has an EV. The bot needs to calculate pot odds, implied odds, and opponent fold equity to make +EV decisions consistently.
→Hand strength evaluation: Accurately assessing current hand rank and equity against likely opponent ranges — the mathematical foundation of every decision.
→Range-based thinking: Rather than assigning one specific hand to an opponent, the bot reasons about the full set of possible hands — a more realistic and powerful model.
→Bluffing and balance: Even bots need to bluff at the right frequency — otherwise opponents can exploit a "only bets strong hands" strategy by simply folding.
