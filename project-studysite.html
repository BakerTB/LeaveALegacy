🌿Overview
→Built a full SAT practice website in JavaScript to create a more active, personalized study experience than passive reading or commercial prep sites.
→Questions are generated by AI based on specific SAT question parameters — covering math, reading, and writing with calibrated difficulty.
→A built-in live timer tracks minutes per question, so you can immediately see where you're spending too long and improve time allocation accordingly.
→Spring 2025 — 4 hrs/week over 2 weeks.
⚡Key Features
AI Question Generation
Prompts an AI model with specific SAT section parameters to generate fresh, calibrated practice questions on-demand — no two sessions are the same.
Live Timer Per Question
A running clock shows exactly how many seconds/minutes you spend on each question. Instant awareness of pacing — the first step to improving it.
Time Management Feedback
After each session, see a breakdown of time-per-question. Identify which question types take longest and where to focus practice.
Speed-Focused Design
Clean, distraction-free UI with everything visible at a glance — the emphasis is on rapid response, not navigating menus.
🎯Why Speed Tracking Changes Everything
→The SAT is as much a time management test as a knowledge test. Running out of time is usually the primary differentiator at high scores.
→Most students don't know exactly where their time goes. By logging seconds per question, you get precise data instead of vague feelings.
→Pattern recognition: If you consistently spend 4 minutes on "systems of equations" but 30 seconds on "word problems," you know which to drill vs. skip on test day.
→The timer creates healthy pressure during practice — simulating test conditions without needing to take full 3-hour exams every session.
